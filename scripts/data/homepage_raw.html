Welcome to Tattoo Villa – Where Art Meets Skin

If you’re looking for the perfect place to get a tattoo that tells your story, Tattoo Villa is where your search ends. Recognized as one of the best tattoo studio in Delhi, we combine creativity, precision, and passion to craft designs that stay with you forever — both on your skin and in your heart.

Founded and led by Anand Sohal and Harminder Sohal, Tattoo Villa has become a name synonymous with excellence in the world of body art. With more than 14 years of experience, Harminder Sohal is celebrated as one of India’s most skilled tattoo artists, having trained over 20 students who are now successfully pursuing their careers across the country.

OUR ARTIST

Harminder Singh

I was born as an artist. I have tried to rescue from what I am many times but I always ended up with a pencil in my hand. Here at Tattoo Artist in Delhi, we offer a wide variety of tattoo styles. I am a master of portraits, skulls, roses and cosmic illustrations. However, I am also skilled in black and grey shading with lasting colour saturation. I am a perfectionist and will work with you to create a tattoo that you will love for the rest of your life.

Jaspreet Singh

I am tattoo artist and graphic designer. I have been drawing, creating ever since I was able to hold a pencil in my hand. My interests are very wide, but tattoos and perfection are closest to my heart. I was interested in creating tattoos in 2014, which I have been doing professionally since then. Currently working as a senior artist at Tattoo Artist in Delhi . My favorites are portraits and realism, but I love to create in every style, because each holds a different challenge.
